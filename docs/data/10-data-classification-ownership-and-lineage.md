## Purpose

Assign ownership, stewardship, custody, classification, visibility, privacy, encryption, retention, export, support and lineage obligations to every logical table.

## Scope

The approved five-level B03 scheme is reused without alteration. A public derivative does not declassify its source; every derivation has an allowlist, current state/Consent policy, source version and purge/rebuild behavior.

## Lineage correction principles

- A current-profile edit creates no implicit Participant Snapshot, Result or Certificate edit.
- A Score Sheet reopening retains the former submission and identifies exact replacement/calculation inputs.
- A Result/Certificate correction appends a successor and keeps public verification of former status.
- A Consent withdrawal removes future/public authority and triggers projection/media actions while protected evidence follows purpose/hold policy.
- Organization, geography and Quran corrections use validity/supersession so historical references remain reproducible.
- A Legacy import never sheds source file, row, checksum, validation, reviewer or provenance classification.
- Audit lineage includes command correlation, authoritative version, canonical event hash, Outbox event and external checkpoint; operational logs do not replace it.

## Governance and review

Classification or ownership change requires the owning module, Privacy/Security/Records review, migration impact, derivative/cache/search purge analysis, backup/export handling and updated traceability. New data cannot enter logs, JSON payloads or projections without purpose, class and owner. QMDB-DCL-005 private keys/secrets are references only and remain in a dedicated secrets/KMS/HSM boundary.

## P3-B02 lineage addendum

Person, name, birth-date, geography and guardianship data are `QMDB-DCL-003` confidential personal data. The People
module is the global owner; no Workspace owns or scopes these B02 records. Audit and notification integrations retain
only bounded action and opaque-reference metadata, never names or birth dates. Browser storage, public caches, public
projections and exports are not part of this batch.

## P3-B05 identity-resolution lineage addendum

Pairing selectors, HMAC verifiers, claims, assertions, duplicate cases, consent decisions, alias mappings, review
references, and associated Person/Account relationships are confidential personal or restricted security data.
Plaintext pairing secrets, names, birth dates, contact values, comparison material, review justification, and
idempotency values are excluded from audit metadata, notifications, URLs/referrers, browser storage, release assets,
and operational logs. The People module owns canonical Person lineage; Organization Affiliation is a constrained
participant only. Retention periods, legal hold, independent review, external identity verification, and appeals
remain open governance decisions.
