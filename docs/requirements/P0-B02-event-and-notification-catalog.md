## Event rules

Domain state is authoritative in its owning module. Events are durable facts or projections of committed facts; consumers are idempotent because delivery is at least once. Public live-update events contain approved projections, never private official-score evidence. Integration and notification failure cannot roll back the source transaction.

## Delivery boundaries

- Domain events: committed lifecycle facts owned by the source aggregate.
- Integration events: minimized/versioned contracts derived from domain facts and delivered through the outbox.
- Audit events: append-only evidence with integrity linkage and narrower access.
- User notifications: non-authoritative delivery intents governed by recipient, channel, preference and mandatory policy.
- Public live-update events: ordered, replayable, status/freshness-labeled projections that may pause or rebuild.
