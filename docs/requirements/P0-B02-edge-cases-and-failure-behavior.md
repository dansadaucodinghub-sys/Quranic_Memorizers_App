## Safety order

When obligations compete or dependencies fail, behavior defaults to protecting official-record integrity, tenant isolation, personal privacy, child safety, Qur’an text integrity, audit evidence and recoverability. Failure never grants broader access, silently changes an official version, invents a rule, reports a projection as authoritative, or treats a retry as a new business command.
