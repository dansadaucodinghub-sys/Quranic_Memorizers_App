## Purpose

Identify retention/disposition decisions without inventing time periods.

## Scope and rule

Every record remains `Policy not yet approved`. Legal, official-record, security and child-safety reviews are decision dependencies, not automatic indefinite retention. Public removal and authoritative disposition are separate actions.
