# P3 People, Privacy, Guardianship and Identity Resolution

People are global records distinct from Accounts and authorization roles. Private access requires an exact active SELF link or an active profile-management Guardianship relationship. Public IDs and registry codes do not grant authority; no public Person directory or deletion service exists.

The QMDB minor threshold is a product-policy parameter, not a legal conclusion. Profile-management relationships do not certify legal guardianship. Claim pairing is HMAC-only, verification assertions grant no Permission and duplicate resolution is explicit, consent-gated, conflict-aware and non-destructive.
