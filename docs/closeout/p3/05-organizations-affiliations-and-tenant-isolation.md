# P3 Organizations, Affiliations and Tenant Isolation

Organizations and Units are Workspace-owned and all Organization/affiliation access is tenant-scoped. Affiliations are distinct from Workspace Membership and their roles grant no application Permission. Pending Person data stays private; consent, lifecycle, leadership step-up, retirement and cross-workspace guards are verified by the P3 integration tests.

No public Organization roster or staff directory is registered, and no competition registration capability exists.
